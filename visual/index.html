Will this let me solve any DSA question?

Most of them — if you practise, not just read. About 9 in 10 interview questions are one of the 55 patterns here, or two of them combined. Once you can spot the pattern, the code is a template you already know.

Reading alone will not get you there. Recognising a pattern when it is hidden inside a story ("a robot on a grid", "courses with prerequisites") only comes from solving problems yourself. The goal is about 150–200 solved problems, each re-solved from memory a few days later.

The honest rule

Reading teaches you the pattern. Solving teaches you to spot it. Re-solving after 1, 3 and 7 days stops you forgetting it.

What stays hard even after all this: some "Hard" problems need a one-off trick (a clever math fact, an unusual data structure). Nobody solves all of those cold. Interviewers mostly ask Mediums, and for those the patterns are enough.

The plan: every pattern in 1 week, then MAANG level

Week 1Learn every pattern. ~7 hours a day, 6–8 lessons a day, one practice problem per pattern. You will recognise all 47 core patterns and know each template.
Weeks 2–4Blind 75 (book chapters 17–18 at the end): 3–4 problems a day, every one tagged with its pattern. Mediums start to feel routine.
Weeks 5–10MAANG level. Harder problems per pattern, timed: Medium in 20–25 min, Hard in 40. ~250 problems total and weekly mock interviews.
Studying full time (8–12 hours a day)?
  • Days 1–4: every core pattern learned (two weeks' worth of lessons fit into four days).
  • End of week 2: the Blind 75 solved once.
  • Weeks 5–6: MAANG-ready: about 250 problems, Mediums in 20–25 minutes, mock interviews passing.
  • 2–3 months: real mastery: 400+ problems, most Hards, calm under pressure.

Past about 8 focused hours a day, extra hours stop helping: memory needs sleep, and re-solving after 1, 3 and 7 days needs real calendar days. Aim for 8–9 hours in 90-minute blocks, not 12 tired ones.

Honest expectation

One week is enough to learn and recognise every pattern. It is not enough to be fast under interview pressure — that comes from the practice weeks. At 3 hours a day after week 1, most people reach the MAANG bar in 2–3 months.

Weeks 2–10 — up to MAANG level

  1. Weeks 2–4: Blind 75 (book chapters 17–18). Try 20 minutes, read the "In plain words" box, try again, then the solution. Re-solve after 1, 3 and 7 days.
  2. Weeks 5–8: depth per pattern. 4–6 harder problems per pattern (NeetCode 150 / LeetCode company tags for Google, Meta, Amazon). Hards in trees, graphs and DP are what MAANG rounds add.
  3. Weeks 9–10: interview mode. Random Mediums, 25-minute timer, talk out loud. Two mock interviews a week. System design and full-stack chapters (book 13–15) 30 minutes a day.
Every day, the same routine

Re-solve what is due (1 / 3 / 7 days old) → learn or watch one pattern → solve new problems → write the pattern name and its one-line trick in a notebook.
